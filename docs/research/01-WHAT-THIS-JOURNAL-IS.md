# What This Journal Is

**Status:** CANONICAL RESEARCH BASELINE  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

This repository is for building an independent English-language intellectual magazine/review.

## Core idea

The publication should be broad in subject but demanding in method.

Its identity should not come from owning one topic. It should come from what it asks writers to do.

> **Take a familiar idea seriously enough that it becomes unfamiliar again.**

A strong piece begins with something readers believe they already understand — a concept, institution, technology, historical story, moral intuition, cultural assumption or scientific claim — and examines it seriously enough that the ordinary description no longer feels sufficient.

The desired reader response is not necessarily “I agree.” It is: “I cannot quite see this subject in the old way anymore.”

## Editorial temperament

The publication should be intellectually challenging and sometimes rebellious.

Rebellious does not mean performatively anti-establishment, partisan, sensationalist or contrarian. It means refusing to grant familiar categories immunity from examination.

> **No topic is too established to question, and no argument is interesting merely because it is provocative.**

## Breadth

Do not narrow the publication to AI, politics, history, Russia, governance or philosophy.

Working editorial territories:

- Ideas & Philosophy
- Politics & Institutions
- Technology & Science
- History
- Culture & Society

These are not yet final site sections.

The design principle is:

**broad subject scope + narrow editorial test.**

## Editorial test

A piece should:

- make a real argument;
- reopen something familiar;
- be constrained by evidence;
- explain why this writer is positioned to make the argument;
- leave the reader with a changed conceptual map.

Internal shorthand:

> **After this piece, is the old category harder to use lazily?**

## What it is not

It is not an academic journal, a personal blog, a vanity publication, a political faction, a generic “magazine of ideas”, a contrarian outlet, or a “hidden knowledge” publication.

## Founder and contributors

Ilias Konstantinidis is Founder & Editor.

The publication should become an institution with external contributors rather than a wrapper around the founder’s own writing. External authors are central to its legitimacy and range.

## Operating hypotheses

Targeted commissioning is likely stronger than waiting for inbound pitches at launch.

A small editorial operation should make decisions quickly and treat contributors’ time respectfully.

Contributor compensation, AI policy, cadence, submissions model, platform, monetization and final section architecture remain open decisions.

## Launch principle

Do not begin with the website.

Before implementation, the project should be able to name the publication, state its proposition in one sentence, identify its first editorial territories, sketch ten pieces it would be proud to publish, identify plausible contributors, and define the launch package.
