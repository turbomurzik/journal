## Current editorial north star

> **Take a familiar idea seriously enough that it becomes unfamiliar again.**

The publication is not currently defined by a narrow subject vertical. Its identity comes from intellectual method and editorial temperament: rigorous arguments that reopen familiar categories, assumptions and descriptions of modern life.

## Important boundary

This is not yet:

- an academic journal;
- a personal blog;
- a vanity publication for the founder;
- a politics-only publication;
- an AI-only publication;
- a history-only publication;
- a contrarian outlet whose purpose is provocation;
- a conspiracy or "hidden knowledge" publication.

The exact public name, launch package, section architecture, contributor policy, AI policy, platform and monetization remain open unless `00-DECISIONS.md` says otherwise.
