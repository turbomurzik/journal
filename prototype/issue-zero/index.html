02 / The editorial position

Question
everything.

We are not contrarians for the sake of noise. We distrust the idea that familiarity confers truth, that institutions are immune to scrutiny, or that fashionable consensus is a substitute for evidence.

Disagree with an established idea. Disagree with us, too. A strong argument earns its place by surviving criticism.

The people behind the ideas

Authors.

We choose writers for original questions and serious thinking, not academic rank, celebrity or institutional prestige.

Write with us

A new voice can change the conversation.

We are interested in doctoral researchers, postdocs, independent thinkers and emerging writers with an idea that makes something familiar look different.

We do not assign conclusions or ask contributors to fill predetermined article titles. Each essay begins with the writer's own argument.

Who we are

Not easily
convinced.

An independent intellectual magazine for people who don't confuse familiar ideas with established truths. Five essays, one question, twice a year.

Question everything.
Then make your case.

We take established ideas seriously enough to challenge them. No theory, institution or argument is above scrutiny, including our own.

We want essays that are bold without being careless. A provocative thought is only the beginning; evidence and reasoning are what make it worth reading.

Young voices.
Independent minds.

We actively seek emerging researchers and writers. Not to tell them what to think, but to help them carry original arguments beyond specialist audiences.

Each number gathers five independently authored essays around one substantive problem. The editorial introduction shows why they belong together, and where they disagree.
