The premise

The world is full of things we think we understand.

What if our definitions are doing more work than we realise? Our opening collection is an invitation to examine what gets counted, what gets left out, and who is allowed to decide. Built around original arguments by emerging researchers and writers.

Terminal patriotism.

What counts as patriotism when faith in the state breaks down, yet loyalty to homeland or comrades endures?

Political philosophy / war
Provisional essay by the Founder & Editor. An original, critically reviewed examination of loyalty, political voice and irreversible risk amid Russia's war against Ukraine. The essay must test competing explanations and never confuse understanding wartime sacrifice with endorsing violence. Not yet written or accepted for publication.
02 / THE EDITORIAL STANDARD

Think
again.Then prove it.

We are not interested in the loudest contrarian claim or the safest fashionable conclusion.

We want arguments that make familiar categories difficult to use lazily. A strong essay changes how its reader sees the subject, and shows why that change is justified.

It should withstand disagreement, represent its evidence fairly and remain readable well beyond its own academic field.

New questions. Original arguments. No predetermined answers.
03 / The next generation of authors

Good ideas do not require tenure.

Our launch programme gives priority to underrepresented and emerging intellectual voices.
The next remarkable essay might come from someone whose name you have never heard.

We seek doctoral researchers, postdocs, early-career scholars, independent thinkers and specialists with a fresh, evidence-led argument. We care about the work, not the prestige attached to the author's name.

We commission specific questions. Authors own their arguments; editors help sharpen structure, clarity, sourcing and the strongest objections.

04 / Practical questions

For prospective contributors.

Do I need an academic appointment?

No. Research quality, originality and evidence matter more than institutional affiliation. Independent researchers and practitioners are welcome within the editorial remit.

Must I already have a complete essay?

No. Our preferred model is editorial commissioning: we discover promising work, discuss an intellectual question with the author and agree the scope before a full draft.

Does the editorial team prescribe the conclusion?

No. An author can disagree with the proposed framing. The editorial process tests reasoning and evidence rather than requiring ideological agreement.

Are commissions currently open?

Not yet. The first issue, compensation policy, invitation process and public name are under development. This preview does not accept submissions or store contact information.
