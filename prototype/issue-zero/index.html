The common question

Who is left out of
the picture?

What happens when people, experiences, or realities fall outside the categories by which institutions recognise the world?

There is no assigned conclusion. Some categories may exclude, others may make justice and knowledge possible. Each author should be free to disagree with the framing.

The editorial method

One issue.
Many minds.

Each edition is built around a question broad enough to contain genuine disagreement, narrow enough to create a real intellectual conversation.

We find distinctive research, invite emerging authors to pitch their own argument, then develop it collaboratively. The closing selection belongs to the quality of the work, not a prefilled editorial grid.

One question does not require one answer.

Editorial ethos

Think again.

Take a familiar idea seriously enough that it becomes unfamiliar again. We commission original public essays, not scripted answers or lightly repackaged academic abstracts.

01 / Discover

A new voice.

Find impressive emerging researchers by the originality of their work, without choosing them to fill a predetermined headline.

02 / Develop

Author first, thesis next.

Invite a short author-led pitch. Only then agree an argument, evidence base, revisions and timeline. An author can disagree with our issue question.

No published issues, no active submissions portal, no promises of audience or launch dates. Planned honoraria, print copies and release frequency remain to be formally approved and costed. This is an editorial design prototype and does not send messages or collect data.
